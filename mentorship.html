The Full Journey

The Mentorship

The 5-Week Private Mentorship

Five weeks  ·  Five private 2-hour sessions  ·  Between-session support

$1,100

This is where we move from curiosity into practice. Over five weeks, we meet privately once a week for approximately two hours. Each week builds upon the one before it, combining teaching, experiential practice, conversation, and personalized exercises for you to explore between sessions.

This is not simply information to learn. You practice it. You experience it. You begin discovering how it works through you.

Week 1

Grounding + Energetic Protection

Before we begin opening channels or exploring subtle energy, we create a foundation. We explore grounding practices, energetic boundaries, protection, clearing, and ways of intentionally opening and closing your energetic practice.

We also create an intention for your five-week journey and incorporate personalized Alqvimia energies into the opening of your container. You’ll leave with grounding and protection practices to work with throughout the week.

Week 2

Intuition + Pendulum Work

Before learning to channel, we begin developing your relationship with your own internal signals. We explore intuition, discernment, self-trust, and the ways your system communicates through subtle sensation, inner knowing, emotional clarity, and energetic response.

We also work with the pendulum as a tool for exploration and practice, including establishing your responses, learning how to ask clear questions, and recognizing when the pendulum is reflecting energy, expectation, or clarity.

Week 3

Channeling

With grounding, protection, and discernment underneath you, we begin exploring channeling more intentionally. We look at how you prepare yourself, set intention, create boundaries around the practice, open and close your channel, and begin recognizing the different ways information may come through.

We practice together and explore how channeling feels specifically for you rather than trying to force your experience into someone else’s framework. We also discuss discernment, responsibility, and working intentionally with whatever you experience.

Week 4

Light Language + Soul Expression

This week is about opening the voice as a channel for energy, expression, and non-linear communication. We begin with breath, sound, and vocal release practices that help soften control and loosen the habitual patterns of the thinking mind.

From there, we explore what is often referred to as light language, a form of intuitive vocal expression that arises from energetic and soul-level connection rather than learned linguistic structure. We focus on connecting with what can be understood as your soul language, the unique energetic signature of expression that already exists within you.

Week 5

Energy Work + Clearing

Our final week brings many of the previous practices together through energetic awareness and self-practice. We explore sensing and scanning your own body, noticing areas that feel dense, activated, or stuck, and experimenting with different ways of moving and clearing energy.

We also explore energetic hygiene, clearing after intense experiences, and practices for returning to yourself. The emphasis begins with learning to work with your own energy first, creating a strong personal foundation. From there, we begin to gently explore how this awareness can extend outward into working energetically with others.

We close by reflecting on what has opened for you, which practices feel most natural, and where you feel called to continue developing.

Between-Session Support

The mentorship includes messaging support throughout our five weeks together. This space is specifically for questions and guidance related to the practices you are exploring between sessions.

Because much of this work happens through experience, questions often arise once you begin practicing on your own. Rather than waiting until our next session, you have support as you integrate what is unfolding.
