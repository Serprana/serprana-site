An Invitation

“You’re not looking for someone to convince you that this world exists. There is already a knowing within you.”

This mentorship is for those who already feel a connection to channeling, energy work, and their intuitive or energetic abilities, and are ready to learn how to work with them in a grounded, intentional way.

You may not know where to begin, how to develop those abilities, or how to navigate them responsibly. What you’re looking for is guidance. A space to explore, ask questions, learn foundational practices, and develop greater confidence and discernment.

Together, we can explore practices such as grounding, energetic protection and clearing, channeling, light language, connecting with spirit, moving and sensing energy, energetic boundaries, discernment, pendulum work, and strengthening your intuition, with the work personalized around what is naturally opening for you.

This is less about following a rigid spiritual system and more about helping you develop your own relationship with your abilities, with grounding, responsibility, discernment, and self-trust at the foundation.

Where It Begins

Begin With an Exploration Session

Exploration Session

60 minutes  ·  1:1  ·  in-person or virtual

$110

This first session is not a casual introduction. It is the beginning of your work.

We use this time to understand what is already active within you, what is trying to emerge, and what kind of support would actually serve your development. Many people come into this work already experiencing intuitive signals, energetic sensitivity, spontaneous channeling, or a sense that something is “opening” without knowing how to structure or trust it. This session helps bring clarity to that experience.

We’ll explore your experiences, the abilities you already sense within yourself, what has been naturally emerging, and what you are most curious to develop. I’ll also share how I work, the principles behind this mentorship, and what it looks like to develop these abilities in a grounded and sustainable way.

By the end of this session, you will have a clearer understanding of:

  • What is already active in your system
  • What direction your development is naturally moving toward
  • What practices would support you right now
  • Whether continued mentorship would be supportive for you

If you choose to continue, this session becomes the foundation for your personalized 5-week mentorship. If you choose not to, you still leave with clarity, language for your experience, and a grounded sense of next steps.

The Full Journey

The 5-Week Private Mentorship

Five Private 2-Hour Sessions

Five weeks  ·  Between-session messaging support included

$1,100

This is where we move from curiosity into practice. Over five weeks, we meet privately once a week for approximately two hours. Each week builds upon the one before it, combining teaching, experiential practice, conversation, and personalized exercises for you to explore between sessions.

This is not simply information to learn. You practice it. You experience it. You begin discovering how it works through you.

You’ll also have between-session messaging support for questions that arise specifically around your weekly practices.

Week by Week
1

Grounding + Energetic Protection

Before we begin opening channels or exploring subtle energy, we create a foundation. We explore grounding practices, energetic boundaries, protection, clearing, and ways of intentionally opening and closing your energetic practice. We also create an intention for your five-week journey and incorporate personalized Alqvimia energies into the opening of your container.

2

Intuition + Pendulum Work

Before learning to channel, we begin developing your relationship with your own internal signals. We explore intuition, discernment, self-trust, and the ways your system communicates through subtle sensation, inner knowing, emotional clarity, and energetic response. We also work with the pendulum as a tool for exploration and practice, including establishing your responses, learning how to ask clear questions, and recognizing when the pendulum is reflecting energy, expectation, or clarity.

3

Channeling

With grounding, protection, and discernment underneath you, we begin exploring channeling more intentionally. We look at how you prepare yourself, set intention, create boundaries around the practice, open and close your channel, and begin recognizing the different ways information may come through. We practice together and explore how channeling feels specifically for you rather than trying to force your experience into someone else’s framework. We also discuss discernment, responsibility, and working intentionally with whatever you experience.

4

Light Language + Soul Expression

This week is about opening the voice as a channel for energy, expression, and non-linear communication. We begin with breath, sound, and vocal release practices that help soften control and loosen the habitual patterns of the thinking mind. From there, we explore what is often referred to as light language, a form of intuitive vocal expression that arises from energetic and soul-level connection rather than learned linguistic structure. We focus on connecting with your soul language, the unique energetic signature of expression that already exists within you.

5

Energy Work + Clearing

Our final week brings many of the previous practices together through energetic awareness and self-practice. We explore sensing and scanning your own body, noticing areas that feel dense, activated, stuck, or tangled, and experimenting with different ways of moving and clearing energy. We also explore energetic hygiene, clearing after intense experiences, and practices for returning to yourself. The emphasis begins with your own energy first, creating a strong personal foundation. From there, we gently explore how this awareness can extend outward into working energetically with others.

We close by reflecting on what has opened for you, what you’ve learned about yourself, which practices feel most natural, and where you feel called to continue developing.

Between Sessions

Messaging Support

The mentorship includes messaging support throughout our five weeks together. This space is specifically for questions and guidance related to the practices you are exploring between sessions.

Because much of this work happens through experience, questions often arise once you begin practicing on your own. Rather than waiting until our next session, you have support as you integrate what is unfolding.

Messaging support is not intended to replace our sessions or provide on-demand or emergency support. Deeper exploration and processing are held within our scheduled time together.
